# Dayforge: Planner, Habits & Money

Turn your daily habits into a game. Track daily quests (read, journal,
meditate, move, ...), bigger weekly quests, and long-term goals you're
chasing across months and years — all with XP, levels, streaks, and
unlockable achievements.

## Features

- **First-run setup** — four quick steps: what you want to get better at
  (plus your name if the account didn't give one), your day (wake, bed,
  school or work hours), your character and app colour, then the personal
  Monday–Sunday plan it builds. Time boxes never overlap, even on small phones.
- **Sleep** — a night-mode screen from Today: your ideal sleep by age
  (AASM/CDC ranges, adjustable), a bedtime/wake-up calculator in ~90-minute
  cycles, sleep sounds made on the phone (rain, waves, fan, brown and pink
  noise) with a timer, a sleep log with a 7-night chart and advice, and
  research-backed tips. Logging a night can tick "Get 7+ hours of sleep" and
  "Bed before 23:00" quests.
- **Money modes** — Money → Save: Balanced (50/30/20), Save hard, Big goal,
  Safety net, Pay off debt, Grow it, Enjoy now. Each sets the save-first
  share and what to focus on. Plus 13 proven saving methods (some set
  themselves up, like an emergency fund or the 52-week challenge) and scam
  red flags.
- **Daily rewards** — open the app each day and claim the next of seven
  rewards (shards, a Quest Swap, a Streak Shield, a prize chest on day 7).
  Missing a day never takes progress away; seven days in a row adds a
  50-shard bonus. The card always shows tomorrow's reward.
- **Easy reading** — Settings → Appearance: Normal, Roomy or Extra makes
  gaps, text and line spacing bigger and shows full names instead of "…".
- **About, terms & privacy** — Settings → About explains what the app is for,
  how it works, and short plain-language terms and privacy.
- **Today home screen** — a greeting, a "Right now / Next" card from your
  plan with one-tap Done and Focus, quick actions, and a daily mood
  check-in.
- **Today at a glance** — the top of Today shows today's progress ring,
  XP to the next level, the streak with this week's days (consistency, not
  only perfection) and an "Up next" button that finishes the next quest in
  one tap (or jumps to it when it needs input).
- **Rest days** — miss one day in any seven and the streak carries on (it
  just doesn't grow that day). Two missed days in a row reset it, unless a
  Streak Shield covers them; shields aren't spent on rest days.
- **Safe data** — a save that can't be read is copied aside before anything
  is written over it, one automatic snapshot per day is kept for the last
  three days (Settings → Data → Automatic backups → Restore, with undo),
  and a banner warns if the phone's storage is full.
- **Subscription calculator** — weekly, monthly and yearly subscriptions in
  one place with what they cost per month, per year and per day, biggest
  first, plus the check-up for ones you hardly use. Bills have their own
  list; both count toward "fixed costs".
- **Manual assets** — crypto, stocks and ETFs, gold and anything else, with
  amounts and prices you type in (no exchanges, wallets or price services);
  profit or loss, a split by type and a reminder when prices get old. The
  old live-price code is kept for a possible future opt-in.
- **Big moments** — finishing a quest bursts with a shockwave and sparks, a
  perfect day and a level-up get full-screen moments; everyday screens stay
  calm, and Reduce motion turns all of it down.
- **Dayforge icons** — the app's own two-tone icons for the tabs, streak,
  shards, XP, chests, shields and quick actions.
- **Rewards shop** — your own real-life treats (30 minutes of gaming, a
  movie night…) with a price in shards; buy them when earned, with undo
  and a history. Shards come from quests and are shared with the wardrobe.
- **Quest streaks** — each daily quest shows its own streak and how many of
  the last 30 days it was done.
- **Monthly budget (envelopes)** — money in minus fixed costs and saving is
  "Ready to assign"; split it into category envelopes (or fill them from
  daily limits / evenly) and see what's left in each, per day.
- **Top 3 to-dos** — star up to three to-dos as today's priorities; they
  go to the top.
- **Quick add** — one box on Today for everything, in plain words: "Dentist
  tomorrow 15:00" goes to the plan, "spent 4.50 on lunch" logs an expense,
  "every day: read 10 pages" makes a quest, "gym every monday 17:30" adds a
  weekly plan item, anything else becomes a to-do. It shows what it
  understood before you add it. Also on the app icon's long-press menu.
- **Wishlist** — things you want one day, with price and link. Each shows how
  many weeks of saving it takes with your money plan, asks you to wait a day
  before buying, turns into a savings goal with one tap, and the whole list
  can be shared (handy before birthdays).
- **Breathe** — a 1, 3 or 5-minute guided breathing session (in 4, hold 4,
  out 6) with a growing and shrinking circle. It counts as focus time and
  ticks off a breathing or meditation quest.
- **Subscription check-up** — go through your subscriptions, say which you
  hardly use, and see what cancelling them saves a year and what that would
  grow to if invested.
- **Your coach** — personal tips from how your days really went (missed
  yesterday, too many quests, too easy, weekend dip, short sleep, low mood,
  no water or movement, no morning routine, comfort zone), each with a
  one-tap fix such as adding the right quest. Runs on the phone only.
- **Daily boost** — a quote of the day on Today, from 1,000+ famous quotes,
  proverbs and original lines, or a Bible verse (King James Version, 230+),
  or both, or hidden (Settings). "Another" shows a random one; share or copy it.
- **Motivation reminders** — 50 different tough-love pushes (never rude) when
  you haven't done a quest by your evening check time, plus a morning boost
  with the quote of the day. While the app is open or in the background they
  come from the app; on Android with the app installed, the service worker
  also sends them with the app closed (periodic background sync). iPhones
  and fully closed apps elsewhere would need a push server.
- **Number quests you can tune** — water shows litres and lets you pick your
  glass or bottle (150 ml to 1 L) and daily goal; other number quests get
  their own goal and quick-add buttons. The title follows the new goal.
- **Money plan and cash counter** — enter what you get each month (pocket
  money or salary) and how much to save; the app takes off bills, then
  spreads your spending over the week using your plan (more on days with
  fun plans, less on quiet days) and reminds you about birthdays. Count
  your notes and coins to set your wallet in one tap.
- **Focus timer** — 15/25/45/60-minute sessions that keep running when
  the app is closed, with a chime, vibration and notification at the end;
  focus minutes show up in Stats.
- **Mood insights** — mood this week, and once there's enough data, what
  seems to lift it (finishing your quests, focus time).
- **Avatar & wardrobe** — a drawn character with 155 items across faces &
  expressions (including "Match my mood", which follows your daily mood
  check-in), skin, hair, hair colour, outfits, hats, eyewear, scenes and
  shoulder pets,
  each Common, Rare, Epic or Legendary. Try anything on, buy with shards
  (earned from XP), unlock milestone gear through levels and streaks, and
  win chest-only items from reward chests (one per perfect day, perfect
  week and level-up).
- **Appearance** — the futuristic Nebula look by default (with a Space
  Grotesk heading font), eleven accent colours and eight backgrounds (Nebula,
  Aurora, Ember, Deep sea, Candy, Graphite, Midnight, true Black) in
  Settings, applied before first paint.
  A **light (white) theme** and an **Automatic** mode that follows the phone.
- **Settings** — opened from the gear in the header or on the You tab, laid
  out like a phone's settings app: profile, appearance, language, plan
  reminders, sounds & vibration, reduce motion, launch animation, quests,
  account, data & backup, install and about.
- **29 languages** — every screen's main words are translated, and dates,
  weekdays, times and money follow the chosen language (12/24-hour switch).
- **Diary & notes** — a private ruled diary page for every day (with writing
  prompts, word count and your mood), past pages by month, and free notes
  with colours, pinning, search and undo for deletes.
- **Continue with Google** — sign in with a Google account on the first
  screen or in Settings → Account. Progress is backed up to the app's hidden
  folder in your own Google Drive (no access to your files or email), backs
  up automatically when you leave the app, and comes back when you sign in on
  a new phone. Needs an OAuth web client ID: set `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID_DEFAULT` in
  `src/lib/google.ts` (or `VITE_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` at build time); without it
  the buttons show "coming soon".
- **Friends** — add friends by code or invite link, see what they finish
  (quests, runs, streaks, levels, badges) with pop-up notifications, a weekly
  XP leaderboard and 👏 cheers. You choose what's shared; money, plans, diary
  and notes are never shared. Runs on Firebase (setup in
  `SETUP-GOOGLE-AND-FRIENDS.md`, rules in `firestore.rules`).
- **Daily streak like Duolingo** — one quest a day keeps the streak; the
  first quest each day shows a full-screen fire with the number rolling up
  and the week filling in (milestones at 3, 7, 14, 30, 50, 100, 365 days).
- **Easy for first-timers** — Simple mode (on by default, switch in
  Settings) keeps each screen to its main cards. New users start with 4
  daily quests matched to their goals (the rest can be switched on in
  Settings → Quests), a "Getting started" checklist on Today, a one-time
  ⓘ button on each tab (explanations only show when tapped), and a
  plain-words "How it works" page.
- **Skip Pass** — a rare prize (6% in reward chests, 3% in the daily box)
  that skips one daily quest: it counts as done for your streak and perfect
  day, but gives no XP and doesn't unlock the daily box.
- **One-time events** — add an appointment, test or birthday for a specific
  date ("Only once"); it shows on that day, appears in a "Coming up" list,
  goes into the calendar file as a single event, and disappears by itself
  once the day is over. The day picker shows this week's dates.
- **Money tips, bills and crypto** — the Money tab has Overview, Crypto and
  Tips. Tips come from your own numbers (top category, small buys, limits,
  savings cushion, subscriptions, crypto share), plus 16 money lessons, a
  "watch your money grow" compound calculator and an "is it worth it?"
  calculator. Overview adds this month's spending by category and a bills &
  subscriptions tracker with due dates. Crypto tracks 22 popular coins (or
  any coin with a typed price) with live prices from CoinGecko, 24h change,
  profit/loss and the basics to know before buying.
- **Five tabs** — Today, Plan, Money, Quests (weekly quests + long-term
  goals) and You (profile, stats, badges, diary).
- **Accounts on the device** — create an account with an optional 4-digit
  PIN, "remember me", several accounts per phone (each with its own data),
  switch / sign out / delete. Existing saves move into the first account.
- **Number quests & running** — step, water, push-up, page and minute
  goals that complete at their target, plus a GPS run tracker (distance,
  time, pace, keeps the screen awake, filters GPS jumps) that feeds
  distance quests; runs can also be added by hand.
- **Real answers for written quests** — "Gratitude x3" needs three
  different answers with real words, journals need 10 real words, so a
  "." no longer completes them.
- **Daily box** — unlocks after your first quest of the day, opens once a
  day: shards, an XP boost for the day (+50% or double), a Quest Swap
  token, a Streak Shield (covers a missed day automatically), a bonus
  chest, or (about 2%) a Mythic daily-box-exclusive cosmetic.
- **Weekly recap** — your week in numbers in Stats, and last week's recap
  on Today every Monday.
- **Undo** for deletes, **offline support** and an **install guide** for
  putting the app on your home screen, plus home-screen shortcuts.

- **Daily quests** that reset every day — small, repeatable habits worth
  quick XP.
- **Week Plan** — a reusable Monday–Sunday routine (no dates). Add
  timed items like "7:00 Wake up", "7:15 Meditation", tap any item to
  edit it, copy a whole day to other days, and tick things off — ticks
  clear automatically each new week while the plan stays. Today's slice
  shows up on the Today tab. Colour-code items (Health, Mind, Study,
  Work, Personal), start from a ready-made routine, and get reminders:
  export the plan to your phone's calendar as weekly repeating events
  with alerts, or get pop-up alerts while the app is open.
- **Money** — enter what you have in spending money, savings, and
  investments and see it as a ring chart with the total in the middle,
  split into "can spend" and "don't touch". Set a daily spending limit
  (or have it worked out from how many days your money must last),
  split across categories like food, transport, and fun; log what you
  spend and see what's left today, a warning before you go over, a
  7-day history, and a one-tap "move today's leftover to savings".
  Savings goals (like a car) each get their own fund in savings, with a
  progress bar and an estimate of when you'll reach them at your weekly
  saving rate.
- **Weekly quests** — bigger challenges you have all week to finish.
- **Long-term goals** — track progress toward yearly/monthly/custom
  targets (books read, meditation days, savings, anything with a number).
- **XP, levels, and titles** that grow as you complete quests.
- **Streaks** for consecutive "perfect days" (every daily quest done).
- **21 unlockable achievements** for streaks, totals, levels, and goals.
- **Activity heatmap** and stats dashboard to see your history at a
  glance, even months later.
- **Fully customizable** — add, edit, archive, or delete quests and
  goals to fit your own habits.
- **Local-first** — all progress is saved in your browser's storage.
  Export a JSON backup any time from the Manage tab, and import it to
  restore or move to another device.

## Getting started

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Then open the printed local URL in your browser.

## Build

```bash
npm run build
```

Outputs a static site to `dist/` — deployable anywhere that serves
static files (Netlify, Vercel, GitHub Pages, etc).

## Tech

React + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS, with Lucide line icons. No backend — everything runs
client-side and persists to `localStorage`.
